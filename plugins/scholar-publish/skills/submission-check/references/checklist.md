# Submission checklist

Answer each row with Pass, Fail, Not applicable or Unknown. Quote the venue rule (short) and name where the evidence
is. A row you cannot verify from the venue's instructions and the project's files is Unknown, not Pass.

## 1. Venue and format
- [ ] Venue, article type and deadline confirmed from the instructions (version and date noted).
- [ ] Required structure present (sections, abstract type, keywords, running title) and in the required order.
- [ ] Word, page, figure and table limits met (what counts: abstract, references, appendices, captions?).
- [ ] File format, template, fonts, margins, line numbers and page layout as required.
- [ ] Title, abstract and keywords follow the venue's rules; a frozen title or abstract was not changed.
- [ ] Citation and reference style is the venue's (APA 7 only if the venue or university says so).
- [ ] Headings, numbering, equations, tables and captions follow the venue's conventions.
- [ ] Language and spelling follow the venue (US or British; serial comma); overrides recorded in `scholar.json` and decisions.
- [ ] Prose lint run with the venue's settings; remaining hits judged by the scholar.

## 2. Evidence and references
- [ ] `check-citations.mjs` clean: every citekey resolves, no raw URLs in prose, quotations carry locators.
- [ ] Reference list complete and authentic: authors, year, title, venue, DOI or URL where one exists.
- [ ] Every in-text citation is in the list; every listed item is cited or deliberately kept (venue rule on uncited items).
- [ ] No `[GAP:` markers left (build preflight with `--submission`).
- [ ] Claims in the paper have status `claim-checked` or `approved-for-use`, or are written as interpretation or hypothesis.
- [ ] Status and tense are accurate: ongoing work described as ongoing, approvals and data collection stated only when documented.
- [ ] Opposing evidence is cited where the claim is contested.

## 3. Figures, tables and rights
- [ ] Every figure is `approved` or `manuscript-ready` in its sidecar (or is a labelled concept sketch).
- [ ] Figure files meet the venue's size, resolution, colour and format rules; text in figures is legible at print size.
- [ ] Alt text or an accessible description where the venue asks for it; colour is not the only carrier of meaning.
- [ ] Rights and permissions for every reused or third-party figure, table, image and long quotation are in hand, with credit lines in the venue's form.
- [ ] Axes, units, sample sizes and time coverage are accurate; no data altered, interpolated or hidden.

## 4. Ethics, consent and integrity statements
- [ ] Ethics approval number and body stated, if human participants or personal data are involved (from the project records, not memory).
- [ ] Consent and confidentiality statements as the venue requires; no identifiable participant detail in the manuscript or files.
- [ ] Funding, conflicts of interest, acknowledgements and author contributions completed by the authors.
- [ ] Authorship and order agreed by all authors; corresponding author and ORCID details as required.
- [ ] Research integrity declarations (originality, not under review elsewhere) match the facts.

## 5. AI-use disclosure
- [ ] The venue's and the institution's AI policies read (version and date).
- [ ] The AI assistance used in this project is listed (drafting help, editing tools, Humanizer, translation, code, figures).
- [ ] The required disclosure statement exists, in the venue's required place and form, written by the scholar.
- [ ] No text, figures or analysis generated by AI are presented as something else, and none are against the policy.

## 6. Data and code availability
- [ ] Data availability statement present in the venue's form; true to what is deposited or restricted.
- [ ] Repository, DOI or access conditions exist and work, or the reason for restriction is stated.
- [ ] Code, specifications and sidecars needed to reproduce figures are available as promised.
- [ ] No confidential, participant or licensed material in anything that will be public.

## 7. Review process
- [ ] Anonymisation for review (if blind): author names, affiliations, acknowledgements, self-citations in the first person, file metadata, tracked changes, comments, repository links and funding details handled as the venue requires.
- [ ] Supplementary files, cover letter and suggested reviewers prepared if asked.
- [ ] Preprint, thesis-repository and prior-conference-version rules checked against the venue's policy.

## 8. Overlap with the scholar's other publications
- [ ] Reuse record up to date: text, data, figures and analyses reused from the thesis or other papers.
- [ ] Verbatim or near-verbatim overlap identified, cited or rewritten as the venue allows.
- [ ] The paper's new contribution is stated and is not already published elsewhere.
- [ ] Companion papers and the thesis are consistent with this one (claims, numbers, definitions).

## 9. Final steps (the scholar does these)
- [ ] The scholar has read the final PDF or file the venue will receive, not only the source.
- [ ] Submission, upload or email done by the scholar; confirmation saved to the project.
- [ ] Status and handoff updated: what was submitted, when, to whom, which version.

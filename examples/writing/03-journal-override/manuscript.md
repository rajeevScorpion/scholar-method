# Methods

We analyzed borrowing records from 12 branches and organized the members into three groups: students, workers, and retirees. The color of each branch marker on the map shows its opening hours. Earlier work found that evening hours raised borrowing by 18% (@narang2019, p. 12), and the authors describe the result as "an association, not a settled cause" (@narang2019, p. 13).
